Institutional Archival Policies & Standards

Governance framework, bibliographic schema, Dublin Core metadata standards, and intellectual property compliance rules governing the Taguig City University Digital Theses & Dissertations Repository (TCU-IR).

ISO 15836 Compliant
CHED CMO Standards • RA 8293

1. Institutional Repository Charter

The Taguig City University Digital Theses and Dissertations Repository (TCU-IR) is the designated digital archive for scholarly and capstone research produced by students, postgraduates, and faculty of Taguig City University.

In accordance with the guidelines set by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) for higher education research programs and Philippine local universities and colleges (LUCs), TCU-IR fulfills the following institutional mandates:

  • Perpetual Digital Preservation: Archiving defended manuscripts, capstones, and dissertations in redundant, verifiable electronic formats.
  • Academic Transparency & Scholarly Dissemination: Promoting civic and technological advancements by making university research discoverable to authorized researchers, faculties, and municipal agencies.
  • Authenticity & Integrity Safeguards: Ensuring all deposited manuscripts have passed defense panel review, dean endorsement, similarity indexing, and catalog accessioning.

2. Bibliographic Protocols & Dublin Core Schema

All records in TCU-IR are cataloged according to the Dublin Core Metadata Element Set (ISO Standard 15836), providing structured interoperability with academic search engines, institutional harvesters, and Google Scholar.

Dublin Core Element TCU-IR Repository Implementation Cataloging Protocol
DC.title Full Title & Subtitle of the Defended Manuscript Title case as approved by Dean's Office
DC.creator Primary Researchers / Student Authors Inverted name format (Surname, Given Name)
DC.contributor Faculty Research Adviser & Panel Examiners Includes academic titles & collegiate department
DC.subject Library of Congress Subject Headings (LCSH) Controlled vocabulary assigned by Chief Librarian
DC.identifier LCC Call Number & Persistent Handle URI E.g. QA76.76.A65 / hdl:10427/TCU-2024-001
DC.rights Philippine Copyright Law (RA 8293) Educational in-library use; commercial use prohibited

3. Intellectual Property Rights & View-Only Safeguards

Under Republic Act No. 8293 (The Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines), student researchers and faculty advisers retain moral and authorship rights over their original theses and technical inventions.

Non-Distributive Policy

Raw PDF manuscript downloads are disabled for general public access to protect original research from unauthorized duplication or plagiarism before formal patent or journal publication.

Dynamic Canvas Watermarking

The integrated manuscript reader dynamically projects session watermarks (timestamp, active patron identity, and repository accession code) across each page render.

4. Manuscript Accessioning Lifecycle

Every study indexed in TCU-IR progresses through a strict multi-tiered review pipeline before being accessible via the public catalog:

1

Defense Panel Approval & Dean Endorsement

The thesis manuscript is defended successfully, revised according to panel feedback, and signed by the faculty adviser, panel chair, and college dean.

2

Similarity & Integrity Verification

The digital manuscript undergoes automated similarity analysis. The institutional standard requires an overall similarity index of under 15% (excluding citations and bibliography).

3

Chief Librarian Cataloging & LCC Classification

A registered professional librarian verifies the Dublin Core bibliographic metadata, assigns an official Library of Congress Call Number (e.g. QA76.76), and indexes appropriate LCSH subject terms.

4

Accessioning, Handle Generation & Public Indexing

The record is issued an official accession identifier (TCU-ACC-YYYY-XXXX), rendered into the OPAC search index, and made discoverable to researchers across collegiate faculties.
