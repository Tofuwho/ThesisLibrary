Taguig City University

University Library & Digital Archives System

Institutional Purpose

The TCU Theses Library Archiver serves as the central digital intellectual repository of Taguig City University. Designed to preserve, catalog, and disseminate the scholarly research conducted by undergraduate seniors, graduate researchers, and academic faculty across all collegiate departments.

Core Technological Safeguards

View-Only Protection

Prevents unauthorized distribution of thesis manuscripts through disabled direct downloads and dynamic canvas-level watermarking.

Real-Time Deep Search

Combines RapidFuzz indexing with database full-text matching to identify relevant studies across titles, keywords, and abstracts.

Role-Based Governance

Multi-tiered permission hierarchy separating Administrators, Librarians, Faculty Evaluators, and Student Authors.
