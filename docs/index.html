TCU Archiving Policy & Guide

Standards for Capstone & Thesis Submissions

1. Document Requirements

All manuscripts must be submitted in standard PDF format including signed approval sheet, abstract, chapters 1-5, and complete references.

2. View-Only Anti-Leak Protection

Published manuscripts are viewable only within the university reader with watermarking to prevent unauthorized redistribution.

3. Approval Workflow

Submissions undergo faculty adviser vetting, followed by college dean endorsement and final librarian accession cataloging.
